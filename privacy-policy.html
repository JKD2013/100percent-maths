Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Who we are

100Percent is a maths education platform operated for Gordon's School and partner institutions. We are committed to protecting the privacy of students, teachers, and school staff.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: email address, full name, role (student or teacher), school affiliation.
  • Learning data: answers to questions, scores, XP, streaks, badges earned, mastery levels, and time spent on tasks.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, device and browser information (anonymised).

3. How we use your data

  • To deliver personalised maths homework and adaptive tasks.
  • To track progress, award XP and badges, and generate mastery reports.
  • To allow teachers to view their students' progress and assign work.
  • To authenticate accounts and secure the platform.

4. Student data safeguarding

We do not sell student data. We do not use student data for advertising or marketing. Student learning data is visible only to the student, their teachers, and authorised school administrators. We comply with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

5. Data storage

Data is hosted on Supabase (a secure, SOC 2-compliant cloud platform). All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@100percent.example. Student accounts can be deleted by their teacher or school administrator.

7. Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication and optional analytics cookies (which you can decline). No third-party advertising cookies are used.
